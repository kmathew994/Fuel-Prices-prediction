## Detailed description
NSW Fuel Prices helps you find the cheapest nearby petrol station in seconds.

- Enter any NSW postcode to see stations within a 5km radius
- Results are color-coded green (cheapest third), amber (mid-range), and red (priciest third)
- Filter by brand or fuel type (U91, E10, P95, P98, diesel, and more)
- Sorted cheapest-first, with distance and last-updated time for every station
- Data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes from the NSW Government's official FuelCheck API

No sign-up, no tracking, no ads — just a fast, free way to check fuel prices before you fill up.

Source data: NSW FuelCheck (data.nsw.gov.au)
Open source: https://github.com/kmathew994/Fuel-Prices-prediction
